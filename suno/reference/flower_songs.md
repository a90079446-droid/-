# Flower 레퍼런스 → 완성곡 3개

## 1. Golden Minute
- 구조: 데일리형 (Intro → V1 → Pre → 사비 → V2 → 사비 → 브릿지 → 핵사비 → Outro)
- 스타일: 스타일 1 (원곡 결, 스무스 소울 팝) · 99 BPM · 약 3:00
- 화자: 늘 시계를 먼저 보던 남자가 처음으로 시간을 붙잡지 않는 밤

**② 가사 (Lyrics)**
```
[Intro: Short instrumental, Rhodes chords and brushed drums]

[Verse 1: Minimal, Rhodes and bass, low intimate tone]
I'm the one who checks the time
First to leave and first in line
Tonight the terrace holds us still
Glasses resting on the sill
You lean back and close your eyes
I don't reach for my disguise

[Pre-Chorus: Soft strings enter, gentle lift]
Every perfect thing runs out
Let it, just not now

[Chorus: Half energy, Rhodes and jazzy guitar, warm and smooth]
Give me one more golden minute
Let the clock just slip away
Light is resting on your shoulder
Make the ending wait a day

[Verse 2: Brushed drums fuller, guitar licks, relaxed tone]
Used to grip the good things tight
Lost them all before the light
Now I let the moment breathe
Nothing up my folded sleeve
You won't stay and I won't ask
Raise the last inside the glass

[Chorus: Full groove, strings and guitar, warm]
Give me one more golden minute
Let the clock just slip away
Light is resting on your shoulder
Make the ending wait a day

[Bridge: Drums drop out, Rhodes and vocal only]
Sun comes up, it always will
Let it find us sitting still
Some things end so they can stay
Kept the way we were today

[Final Chorus: Full strings and drums, warmest peak, doubled vocals]
Give me one more golden minute
Let the clock just slip away
Light is resting on your shoulder
Then let morning have its say

[Outro: Rhodes and soft strings only]
```

## 2. Do Not Disturb
- 구조: 데일리형 (Intro → V1 → Pre → 사비 → V2 → 사비 → 브릿지 → 핵사비 → Outro)
- 스타일: 스타일 2 (시크한 네오소울) · 96 BPM · 약 3:00
- 화자: 회사를 쥐고 있는 남자가 문고리에 팻말을 걸고 처음으로 자리를 비운 날

**② 가사 (Lyrics)**
```
[Intro: Short instrumental, muted Rhodes and soft rimshot]

[Verse 1: Minimal, sub bass and vocal, low cool tone]
Suits are pacing past my door
Knocking soft, then knocking more
My assistant texts me every hour
Calendar has lost its power
Silk robe, record turning slow
Let the empire run below

[Pre-Chorus: Drums enter, smoky guitar fill]
Elevator chimes, I let it close
Some invitations end in no

[Chorus: Half energy, Rhodes and sub bass, cool and smooth]
Hang the sign, they can wait
Let the meeting start without me
I've been loud for twenty years
Tonight the quiet pays my fee

[Verse 2: Groove tightens, guitar fills, dry deadpan tone]
Thought the building leaned on me
Every floor would fall, you'd see
Monday came, the lights stayed on
Deal was signed while I was gone
Turns out rest is not a crime
Even kings can take their time

[Chorus: Full groove, drums and Rhodes and guitar]
Hang the sign, they can wait
Let the meeting start without me
I've been loud for twenty years
Tonight the quiet pays my fee

[Bridge: Bass and vocal only, close and dry]
Nobody needs a legend
They just need my name
I can sign it Tuesday
Ink will dry the same

[Final Chorus: Key change up a half step, full band, doubled vocals]
Hang the sign, they can wait
Let the meeting start without me
I've been loud for twenty years
Tonight the silence works for me

[Outro: Muted Rhodes and bass only]
```

## 3. CREST
- 구조: 기본형
- 스타일: 스타일 3 (프로젝트 pop DNA) · 99 BPM · 약 3:05
- 주제: 파도 꼭대기의 한순간 / 은유: 몇 년을 기다려 단 한 번 서는 파도의 정점 / 훅: "I'm standin' on the crest"
- 화자: 남들이 다 포기한 바다에서 10년을 기다린 서퍼

**② 가사 (Lyrics)**
```
[Intro: Quiet sung over a soft pad and Rhodes, no drums]
Out where the water stands up like a wall
I paddled for years for a second that tall

[Verse 1: Brushed drums and Rhodes only, no sub bass, rapid rhythmic delivery]
Every morning I was out before the gulls could wake
Salt was in my lungs and every set would break
Arms gave out, the ocean kept on tellin' me no
Sittin' on the board alone and watchin' others go
Everybody wants the ride but nobody wants the wait
Learned to read the water, learned to count the weight
Then the horizon lifted like a curtain on a stage
All the years I waited rollin' in to turn the page

[Pre-Chorus: Mids swell, strings rise, sub bass held back]
Feel the swell come up beneath me now
Hold the line, I know exactly how
Don't you blink, it only lasts a breath
This is why I stayed through all the rest

[Chorus: Heavy sub bass and big drums hit together with lush strings, long soaring notes, doubled vocals]
I'm standin' on the crest
Top of the world for now
Let it rise beneath me
I won't look down
One perfect second high
Worth a thousand falls
If it breaks tomorrow
I still rode it all

[Breakdown: Drums drop out, quiet, only Rhodes]
Hold it, hold it there

[Verse 2: Brushed drums and Rhodes only, no sub bass, rapid rhythmic delivery]
Wave came down and threw me hard against the reef
Came up spittin' sand with blood across my teeth
Crowd up on the beach was sure I'd quit right there
Waxed the board again and gave the sea a stare
Now a kid sits where I sat ten years ago
Shakin' on his board 'cause every set says no
Told him every crest is paid for in the wait
Keep your eyes on the horizon, kid, it's never late

[Pre-Chorus: Mids swell, strings rise, sub bass held back]
Feel the swell come up beneath me now
Hold the line, I know exactly how
Don't you blink, it only lasts a breath
This is why I stayed through all the rest

[Chorus: Heavy sub bass and big drums hit together with lush strings, long soaring notes, doubled vocals]
I'm standin' on the crest
Top of the world for now
Let it rise beneath me
I won't look down
One perfect second high
Worth a thousand falls
If it breaks tomorrow
I still rode it all

[Bridge: Sub and bass stripped out, rapid-fire short phrases]
Paddle and wait
Paddle and wait
Rise up and ride it
Fall, then try it
Out again
Out again
Every crest
Pays the rest

[Final Chorus: Lifts up a half step, loudest peak, heavy sub bass and strings, doubled vocals]
I'm standin' on the crest
Top of the world for now
Let it rise beneath me
I won't look down
One perfect second high
Worth a thousand falls
If it breaks tomorrow
I'd ride it all again

[Outro: Quiet, soft sung reprise over the pad]
Out where the water stands up like a wall
I waited for years and I'd wait for it all
```
