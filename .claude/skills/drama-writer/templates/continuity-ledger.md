# Continuity ledger

Track the **end state** of each shot; it is the start state of the next. Fill only the columns that matter for the scene, but always fill them for any prop or state that the story uses.

## Scene constants: S<NN>
- **Location / layout:** <door left of frame, window behind the table, ...>
- **Time of day / light:** <night; single warm pendant; cold street light through blinds>
- **Weather:** <rain on window, continuous>
- **Wardrobe:** <NAME: charcoal coat, buttoned; NAME: white shirt, sleeves rolled>

## Adjacent-shot check (tick when clean)
- [ ] No action restarts across a cut
- [ ] No prop moves or changes hands without being shown
- [ ] Posture and facing consistent or shown changing
- [ ] Appearance (tears, wet, wounds, clothing) carried over
- [ ] No unexplained time jump (light, weather, completed actions)
- [ ] Every cut has a reason
- [ ] Eyelines and screen direction match positions
- [ ] Nobody appears or disappears from frame without cause
