# THE LAST FITTING - EP 01: Saturday (production)
Language: en · Target: 70 s · Format: vertical 9:16
Video tool: not specified · TTS: provider-neutral tags

**Episode objective:** Arini, a careful seamstress, finishes a routine fitting, and learns that the bride's groom is her own husband. By the end she has chosen not to confront, but to act.
**Opening hook:** Pins between Arini's lips; a bride asks if it's bad luck that the groom has never seen the dress.

## S01 - INT. ARINI'S BRIDAL ATELIER - DAY
Cast: ARINI (34, hair in a low bun, mustard cardigan over a black dress, measuring tape around her neck), SEKAR (26, long loose hair, ivory satin wedding dress, barefoot)
Setting: small workroom; fitting platform center; worktable frame-left with Sekar's phone face up; tall window frame-right, soft daylight; door behind the camera.
Props: pins (in Arini's lips at start), Sekar's phone (on worktable), Arini's phone (in her cardigan pocket), tailor's chalk (on worktable)

### S01-01 · CU · 3s
Arini's face, low in frame, three pins between her lips. Her eyes are on the hem just below frame. Calm. Practised.
Continuity: Arini kneeling, facing the platform; pins in lips.

### S01-02 · MEDIUM · 7s
Sekar stands on the platform in the dress, facing camera. Arini kneels at the hem, back three-quarters to camera.
SEKAR: Is it bad luck if the groom's never seen the dress?
ARINI: [around the pins] Only if he sees it.
Continuity: Arini still kneeling; Sekar's hands loosely together at her waist.

### S01-03 · CU · 5s
Sekar, smiling down at Arini.
SEKAR: He won't. He's never home. [laughs] Sorry. Nerves.
Continuity: Sekar looking down, screen-right.

### S01-04 · CU · 6s
Arini takes the pins from her lips with her right hand. Keeps her eyes on the hem.
ARINI: When's the day?
SEKAR (O.S.): Saturday. Tiny. His family doesn't even know yet.
Continuity: pins now in Arini's right hand.

### S01-05 · INSERT · 3s
Sekar's phone on the worktable lights up: an incoming call. Caller name: "Dimas ♥", with a small photo of a man in a grey suit.
On-screen text: Dimas ♥ (add as overlay in edit)
Continuity: phone face up, on worktable.

### S01-06 · CU · 3s
Arini's eyes move to the phone. Her right hand stops moving. She holds still for two full seconds.
Continuity: pins still in right hand; kneeling.

### S01-07 · MEDIUM · 6s
Sekar steps down from the platform, careful in the dress, and crosses frame-left to the worktable. Arini stays kneeling, looking at the empty platform.
SEKAR: Sorry, I have to-- [into phone] Hi. Yes, I'm at the fitting.
Continuity: Sekar at worktable, phone at her left ear, back half to Arini.

### S01-08 · CU · 5s
Arini, still kneeling. A man's voice comes faintly from the phone. She closes her eyes for a beat.
DIMAS (V.O.): [faint, through the phone] Get the one you like. Don't look at the price.
Continuity: eyes close, then open. Pins in right hand.

### S01-09 · MEDIUM · 6s
Arini stands up, slowly. She puts the pins down on the worktable beside the chalk. Sekar finishes the call and turns back to her, glowing.
SEKAR: He says I should stop worrying about the price.
ARINI: [evenly] He's right.
Continuity: both standing at the worktable; pins on table; Sekar's phone in her left hand.

### S01-10 · CU · 7s
Arini picks up the tailor's chalk. Looks at the dress, not at Sekar.
ARINI: Let's take the waist in a little more. You'll want it perfect for Saturday.
Continuity: chalk in Arini's right hand.

### S01-11 · CU · 4s
Sekar beams, touched.
SEKAR: You're so kind.
Continuity: Sekar facing screen-left toward Arini.

### S01-12 · INSERT · 4s
Arini's own phone, out of her cardigan pocket, in her left hand. Chat with "Dimas". She types, sends: "What time are you home Saturday?"
On-screen text: What time are you home Saturday? (overlay)
Continuity: chalk still in right hand (out of frame); phone in left.

### S01-13 · CU · 4s
Arini, phone lowered. She looks up at Sekar. A small, polite smile. Hold.
Continuity: both phones visible only in previous shots; Arini's smile held to cut.

**Ending beat:** decision (she chooses to act, not to confront), with dramatic irony: Sekar doesn't know who Arini is.

## Production notes
- Two characters maximum in any frame; one main action per shot; static or very slow camera throughout.
- Hand work is limited to single, simple grips (pins, chalk, phone). The pinning itself happens below frame.
- Both phone screens are inserts with text added as overlays in editing, not generated text.
- Dimas is voice only; he isn't seen until a later episode, which saves a character and keeps the reveal on Arini's face.
- Visual reference wording for Arini and Sekar is in the cast line; reuse it in every shot prompt.
