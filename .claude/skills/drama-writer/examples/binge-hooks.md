# Example: hooks that make people keep watching

One premise, worked three ways: the opening hook, the ending cut, and the payoff ladder. All material here is original.

**Premise.** Iris cleans banquet halls at a wedding venue. Five years ago her fiancé, Danny, disappeared a week before their wedding, with her savings. Today she is working a wedding, and the groom is Danny, under another surname. The bride is the venue owner's daughter.

---

## 1. Opening hook

### Weak

```markdown
## S01 - EXT. GRAND MAREN VENUE - DAY
Cast: IRIS

Wide shot of a beautiful wedding venue. Guests arrive. Iris walks through the garden carrying a tray.

## S02 - INT. BANQUET HALL - DAY
Cast: IRIS, DANNY

Iris enters the hall and looks around. She sees the groom across the room.

IRIS: [gasps] It's... him?!
```

Why it fails:
- **Scroll test:** the first frame is a wide, pretty venue. Nothing is wrong yet.
- **Sound-off test:** the whole hook is the gasp and the line.
- **Specificity test:** "she sees her ex at a wedding" opens a hundred dramas.
- **Engine:** curiosity only, and the question ("is it him?") is answered in the same line.
- **Second question:** none. At second 11 the viewer knows everything the hook offered.

### Strong

```markdown
## S01 - INT. BANQUET HALL, SERVICE STATION - DAY
Cast: IRIS, DANNY, SUPERVISOR

Close on a gloved hand polishing a champagne flute. In the curve of the glass, small and upside down, a groom fastens his cufflinks.

The hand stops.

Iris lowers the glass. Across the hall, the groom laughs with the bride's father. On his cuff, a cheap silver cufflink, engraved with a single letter: I.

Iris touches her collar, where a chain disappears under her uniform.

SUPERVISOR: Table one. The groom's champagne. Go.

Iris picks up the tray. She walks toward him. He hasn't turned.
```

Why it works:
- **Scroll test:** the first frame is a hand stopping. Something is wrong before we know what.
- **Sound-off test:** the reflection, the cufflink, the chain carry the hook. The only line is an order that pushes her toward him.
- **Specificity test:** a man still wearing the cufflinks his abandoned fiancée gave him belongs to this story only.
- **Engines stacked:**
  - Curiosity: why is he still wearing them?
  - Anticipation: she is walking toward him with a tray, and we know who she is.
  - Desire: we want him to turn around and see her, in front of the father.
- **Second question:** the supervisor's order opens "what will she do when she reaches him?" before the first question closes.

**The viewer's want by episode 3:** to see Danny recognise Iris in front of the bride's family, and not be able to say why he went pale.

---

## 2. Ending cut

Same scene, two ways to end episode 1.

**After the reaction (closure):**

```markdown
Danny turns. He sees her. The glass slips; champagne runs over his shoes.

DANNY: Iris?
IRIS: Congratulations.

She walks away. He stares after her.
```

The viewer got the recognition, the line and the exit. Strong, but they have what they came for, and nothing forces the tap.

**On the inhale (cut before the answer):**

```markdown
Iris holds out the tray. Danny reaches for a glass without looking.

His fingers stop an inch from hers. He has seen the scar across her thumb.

He starts to look up--
```

Episode 2 opens on his face. The recognition, and "Congratulations", now belong to the next episode, which has to deliver them, and does. This is honest because nothing is reversed: the moment we cut away from is the moment we return to.

---

## 3. Payoff ladder

| Viewer's want | Small payoff | Bigger payoff | Full payoff | Next want it opens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| See Danny exposed | Ep 2: he spills the champagne in front of the father-in-law | Ep 9: the bride finds an old photo in his wallet | Ep 18: Iris reads her old wedding vows at his rehearsal dinner | Who paid Danny to leave five years ago? |
| Get Iris's money back | Ep 5: she learns the venue was bought the month he vanished | Ep 12: she finds the transfer receipt | Ep 24: the owner signs it back to her | What will Iris do with the venue? |
| See the bride learn the truth | Ep 7: the bride notices how Iris looks at him | Ep 15: the bride asks Iris to help her plan the vows | Ep 20: the bride walks out of her own wedding, with Iris | Will the bride become Iris's ally or her rival? |

Each row pays something early, raises the price before the full payoff (by ep 15 Iris likes the bride, so exposing Danny will hurt her too), and opens a bigger want once it is paid.
